Accessibility statement

I want everyone to be able to use this website, including people with disabilities. The site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What is in place

  • An accessibility menu (the pink button in the corner) with text size, high contrast, grayscale, link highlighting, a readable font, text spacing, stopping animations, a large cursor and a focus highlight. Your choices are saved for your next visit.
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a "Skip to main content" link at the top of the page and visible focus outlines.
  • Respect for the "Reduce motion" setting of your device: the welcome bee, the background animation and the sparkles do not move.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • A text version of the 3D skills puzzle for screen readers and for browsers without 3D support.
  • A layout that works on phones, tablets and large screens.

Known limitations

  • The interactive skills puzzle is easiest to use with a mouse or touch screen. The same list of skills is available as text.
  • Some decorative animations are purely visual and carry no information.
